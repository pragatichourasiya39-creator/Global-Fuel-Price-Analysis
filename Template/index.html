About The Project

Turning global fuel price data into meaningful insights

Global Fuel Price Analysis is a data analytics project designed to study how fuel prices vary across different countries and regions.

The project examines petrol, diesel and LPG prices along with taxation, subsidies, income levels and crude oil trends. Interactive dashboards and visualizations help identify patterns, compare countries and understand the factors influencing fuel prices.

Project Objectives

Key questions explored through this analysis

Fuel Price Comparison

Compare petrol, diesel and LPG prices across countries and geographical regions.

Price Trends

Analyze changes in fuel prices over time and examine their relationship with crude oil.

Taxation & Subsidies

Explore how taxation policies and government subsidies relate to fuel prices.
